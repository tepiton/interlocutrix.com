title: How a project runs
type: process
order: 30
steps:
  - title: Listen
    description: "A first week of questions, audits, and awkward silences. We learn what your customers need you to be."
  - title: Explore
    description: "Two or three directions, shown in real situations - not mood boards, actual screens your users would see."
  - title: Refine
    description: "The chosen direction, tuned in tight loops with your team. You see progress every few days, not at the end."
  - title: Ship
    description: "Files, tokens, components, and a recorded handover your future hires can watch. We stay on call for two weeks."
---

No black box, no big reveal. You are in the room the whole time.

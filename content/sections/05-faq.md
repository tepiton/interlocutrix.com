  - q: "What does a project cost?"
    a: "Most engagements land between fifteen and forty thousand dollars, fixed price, scoped after a first call. You get the number before any commitment."
  - q: "How long does a website take?"
    a: "Five to eight weeks, typically. Brand-plus-site runs ten to twelve. We give you a date in the proposal and we keep it."
  - q: "Do you work with our engineers?"
    a: "Yes, and we prefer it. Deliverables are components and tokens, not pictures of components. We speak fluent developer and join your standups when useful."
  - q: "Who owns the work?"
    a: "You do, entirely, on final payment. Source files, fonts (licensed to you), the repository, everything."
  - q: "What if we need something smaller?"
    a: "We keep two audit slots open each month - a fixed-fee, two-week review of what you have, with concrete recommendations. A good place to start."
